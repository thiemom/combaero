- `fanno_channel_flow`'s derivatives are exact for very short ducts, which fixes the compressible `CrossflowSegmentElement` station Jacobian at near-zero segment length (#1019). A choked duct a few microns long carries a flux within ~1e-6 of the sonic one. The choked branch differenced L*(G; Pt0, Tt0, Y) through a re-solved inlet Mach, and those steps crossed sonic: dG/dTt0 came out 17% high at L = 1 um, so the station rows' columns that move the station temperature (the segments' and a hot injection's flows) were 7-17% off. The branch now takes the inlet-Mach limit exactly (Leibniz), as the unchoked branch already did. Below the 1 Pa drive floor, the blend exponent sigma's own variation with Pt0, Tt0 and Y now enters the derivatives too; held fixed, it left dG/dTt0 0.6% off on a 5 cm duct. Flows and residuals are unchanged: only the Jacobian moves.
