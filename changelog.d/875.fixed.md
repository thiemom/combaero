- An `OrificeElement` in a pipe (`in_pipe=True`, a velocity of approach) no longer stops a solve on a spurious reversed flow (#875). Its row solves its own flow consistently with the approach pipe's static pressure. When an outer iterate overshot the flux at which that static pressure falls to the outlet's, that inner solve could run down the mirrored branch to the approach pipe's choke. There it stopped at a reversed flow tens of times the true one, where the row has no pressure slope. It is reachable only when C E beta^2 > 1, i.e. the jet's effective area exceeds the pipe's: a fixed Cd 0.98 at beta 0.95, but also Reader-Harris/Gallagher's Cd near 0.6 at beta 0.95. The inner solve now keeps the root on the side of its drive. Rows that already reached that root are bit-identical. The liner-cooling example's exits converge in their pipes again.
