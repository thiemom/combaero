- **The GUI backend defaults to the compressible regime.** The GUI has always sent `global_regime: "compressible"` for new networks. The backend schema still fell back to incompressible for payloads without solver settings (headless runs, scripts, older clients), so the same drawing solved under different physics. Both now default to compressible. Saved networks keep their saved setting.
  - Compressible channels discharge into a pressure boundary at its pressure and lose their exit dynamic head; incompressible ones end Pt-to-Pt. At low pressure budgets this matters: a jet pump behind an outlet pipe entrains omega 2.23 compressible versus 5.58 incompressible.
