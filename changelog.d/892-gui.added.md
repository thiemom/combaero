- **GUI fuel boundary (#892).** A Fuel Boundary node in the palette: target Tt, lean/rich branch, fuel Tt and composition (CH4 by default), an optional flow guess. A dashed yellow link shows the combustor it controls; drag its link handle onto a combustor, or connect it straight to one. Its card shows the solved fuel flow, and the Inspector shows phi and the combustor's Tt without the fuel. `network_to_gui` exports a `FuelBoundary` as this node; before, it went out as the mass-flow boundary it subclasses, which fixed the fuel flow.
- **Fuel-fixed staged start (#892).** A cold solve with a fuel controller whose air comes through a pressure boundary first holds each fuel flow at its start and solves the flows, then re-sizes the fuel on the air that root carries and solves the controllers from there. The plain cold chain runs only if that fails. Any other failed cold solve with a controller gets the same stage as a retry. The fuel start is sized on a guessed air flow, which on the C3X rig's burner was 2700 times too small: the cold solve stalled. That network now solves in 23.8 s under the GUI's 30 s budget.
